CHAMÁN
Lager beer

Chamán is a branding concept for a Chilean craft golden lager inspired by the country's untamed landscapes and ancestral traditions. The visual identity combines rough edges, bold typography, and the distinctive scenery of the Atacama Desert to create a brand that feels authentic, adventurous, and deeply connected to its origin.

The logotype was intentionally designed with rough, weathered edges, evoking hand-carved wood, worn stone, and the natural erosion found throughout the desert. A white cutout pictogram is integrated into the logo, allowing the surrounding artwork to become part of the mark itself. This creates a strong visual connection between the brand and the landscape, making the logo feel carved directly from the environment. The can features panoramic illustrations of the Atacama Desert, using its iconic mountains, salt flats, and expansive horizons to communicate freedom, exploration, and Chilean identity.

The warm color palette reinforces the beer's approachable character while helping the product stand out on the shelf. Every design decision supports the brand positioning. A golden lager is a clean, accessible beer, so the packaging avoids unnecessary complexity and instead emphasizes honesty, craftsmanship, and nature. The rustic typography, integrated pictogram, and desert imagery work together to express a beer meant for campfires, hiking trails, and outdoor gatherings. Rather than simply decorating the can, these elements tell a cohesive story, making the visual identity and the brand promise inseparable.

ENQUADRE
Therapy CMR

ENQUADRE is a mobile app that helps therapists manage their clients with ease and empathy. It combines scheduling, note-taking, and client management into one warm, human-centered interface.

My goal was to design an experience that feels professional yet personal; helping therapists focus on what truly matters: their clients.

Personas with Interview insights

Therapists juggle multiple clients, sessions, and notes; often across scattered tools. I conducted interviews with independent therapists to uncover pain points in their workflow.

How might we help therapists organize their practice seamlessly while keeping a sense of warmth and humanity in the process?

Some wireframes

I explored different ways to merge scheduling and note-taking into a cohesive workflow. Sketches and wireframes helped refine navigation and visual hierarchy.

The visual direction was guided by warmth, energy, and trust. I used orange and yellow as accent colors, paired with bold black outlines to create a friendly but confident tone.

The final UI focuses on clarity and emotional warmth; minimizing friction for therapists and creating a sense of flow throughout their day.

Some mockups

I prototyped core interactions to make ENQUADRE feel intuitive and alive.

I tested the prototype with 5 therapists and gathered feedback on clarity and navigation. Key improvements focused on simplifying the note-taking flow and improving visibility of upcoming sessions.

ENQUADRE helped demonstrate how warmth and professionalism can coexist in a productivity app.

EUFORIA RADICAL
Brand Identity · eCommerce Content · Art Direction

All clients are, in a way, fun clients. But sometimes life brings you a project that is just a little more fun than the rest, a project you have to accept. Euforia Radical was definitely one of these projects.

The Brief

Sex retail in Chile has an image problem. Most brands communicate through one of two modes: clinical and apologetic, or aggressively loud in a way that feels cheap. Neither reflects what a modern, confident sex shop should feel like. Euforia Radical came to us knowing they wanted something different; they just needed help figuring out exactly what that was.

Mood and colour pallet insights from national and international brands.

Market Analysis

Before touching a single color or typeface, I mapped the landscape. I reviewed the websites and social media presence of Chilean sex shops and a selection of international brands, coding each one across a set of mood and aesthetic dimensions.

The patterns were revealing. Chilean brands clustered heavily around a small set of visual conventions; predictable color palettes, similar logo treatments, and a tone that oscillated between sterile and garish. International brands showed more range, with some carving out genuinely distinct identities in the high-end and intimate categories. The gap in the local market was obvious: there was almost no brand owning a point of view.

That gap became the opportunity.

The Manifesto

With a clear picture of the competitive landscape, I sat down with the owners. Rather than jumping straight into visual exploration, we spent time articulating who Euforia Radical really was; their values, their customer, the experience they wanted to create. That conversation became the brand Manifesto: a written foundation that defined the emotional and aesthetic territory the brand would occupy.

The Manifesto then drove the visual exploration. I developed a range of possible directions; different styles, moods, and visual languages; ach one a genuine answer to what we had written together. This stage is where the brand starts to have a personality, and where clients start to see themselves in the work. After iterating with the owners, we landed on an identity that felt true: bold without being aggressive, sensual without being explicit, confident without being cold.

Some options we explored for the brand's visual identity with the client.

In the end, we landed on a visual identity that was bold without being aggressive, sensual without being explicit, and confident without being cold. We wanted to celebrate the people who use Euforia Radical, not the products they buy. The identity is unapologetically human, playful, and fun; a reflection of the brand's values and the experience it wants to create.

Production & Rollout

Once the identity was locked, the scope expanded into full content production for the brand's commercial launch. This included:

  • Product imagery photographed and edited for third-party eCommerce platforms including eBay, following each platform's technical requirements while maintaining brand consistency.
  • Social media content posts designed for Instagram, aligned to the brand's visual system.
  • Instagram story highlights individual story sets per product category, designed to be pinned as permanent navigation on the brand's profile.
  • Product catalog a complete catalog of all products, produced in both print-ready and digital PDF formats.
  • Purchase guides clear, friendly instructions on how to buy, designed to reduce friction for first-time customers and reflect the brand's welcoming tone.

Every deliverable was an extension of the same brand logic established in the Manifesto; consistent in color, typography, tone, and attitude across every surface.
